Quick answer

If you want one fat separator that does nearly everything well, the OXO Good Grips 4-Cup is our top pick for its thoughtful design and reliable performance.

The Cuisipro 4-Cup is the runner-up for those who prefer a wider base and a slightly faster pour, while the Vremi Fat Separator offers the best value for casual cooks who don't need a large capacity.

A good fat separator is one of those kitchen tools that quietly saves you time and mess. Instead of ladling hot drippings into a bowl and hoping the fat rises, you pour directly into the separator and the spout at the bottom lets you drain off the juices while leaving the fat behind. The result is cleaner gravy, broth, and sauces, and fewer greasy countertops. Choosing the right one comes down to capacity, pour control, and how easy it is to clean. Most hold between 2 and 4 cups, and the best models have a strainer built into the lid, a comfortable handle, and a spout that doesn&#8217;t dribble. Plastic versions are lighter and less expensive, while metal or glass models feel sturdier and often resist staining.
